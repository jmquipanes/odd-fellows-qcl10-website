Why Become an Odd Fellow?

The Independent Order of Odd Fellows (IOOF) is more than a fraternity—it is a journey of personal growth, enduring friendship, and meaningful service. Founded upon the timeless principles of Friendship, Love, and Truth, Odd Fellowship inspires its members to become better individuals while striving to make a positive difference in the lives of others and in the communities they serve.

To join the Order is to become part of a worldwide brotherhood and sisterhood united by shared values, compassion, and a commitment to helping humanity. Through charitable works, mutual support, and community service, Odd Fellows continue to leave a lasting impact across generations.

Odd Fellowship is not simply an organization to belong to—it is a commitment to character, service, and purpose. It calls for dedication, sincerity, and the desire to grow both personally and morally. Yet for those who seek a life of meaning, genuine connection, and a legacy of goodwill, the Independent Order of Odd Fellows offers a truly transformative journey and bonds that last a lifetime.

Are you ready to take the step?

Membership in the Independent Order of Odd Fellows (IOOF) is open to both men and women who are willing to embrace the enduring values of Friendship, Love, and Truth while serving their communities with compassion and integrity.

To become a member, applicants must meet the following simple yet meaningful qualifications:

1. Legal Age – Applicants must be at least 18 years old.

2. Belief in a Supreme Being – Odd Fellowship is non-sectarian and welcomes individuals of all faiths and backgrounds, provided they believe in a higher power.

3. Good Moral Character – Members are expected to uphold honesty, integrity, respect, and good conduct in both their personal lives and within the community, reflecting the noble principles of the Order.

Odd Fellowship provides a welcoming environment where individuals can grow personally, build lifelong friendships, and unite in meaningful acts of service. It is a place where people from different walks of life come together with a shared commitment to charity, unity, and community betterment.

For those seeking a purpose-driven organization founded on fellowship, compassion, and service to humanity, the Odd Fellows offers a rewarding journey and the opportunity to become part of a lasting legacy of good.
