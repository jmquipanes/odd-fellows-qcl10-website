Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Independent Order of Odd Fellows (IOOF)?

The Independent Order of Odd Fellows is one of the world's oldest and largest fraternal organizations dedicated to friendship, love, truth, charity, and community service.

What are the core values of the Odd Fellows?

The core principles of the Odd Fellows are Friendship, Love, and Truth.

Is the Odd Fellows a religion?

No. The Odd Fellows is not a religion or a church. It is a non-sectarian fraternal organization that welcomes people of different faiths who believe in a Supreme Being.

Who can join the Odd Fellows?

Men and women of legal age who are of good moral character and believe in a Supreme Being may apply for membership.

Are women allowed to join?

Yes. Women may join through Rebekah Lodges, and in many jurisdictions, membership opportunities are open to both men and women.

What does the three-link chain symbolize?

The three links represent Friendship, Love, and Truth.

What do Odd Fellows do?

Odd Fellows engage in charitable works, community service, fellowship activities, leadership development, and programs that promote moral and social improvement.

Is the Odd Fellows a secret society?

The Odd Fellows is a fraternal organization with traditional ceremonies and private rituals, but its charitable works, principles, and community activities are public.

What are lodge meetings like?

Lodge meetings usually include opening ceremonies, discussions on charitable and lodge activities, reports, fellowship, and degree work.

How can someone become a member?

An interested person may contact a local lodge, submit an application, meet the qualifications, and undergo the membership process according to lodge rules.

What are the benefits of becoming an Odd Fellow?

Membership offers fellowship, personal growth, leadership opportunities, community involvement, networking, and the chance to make a positive difference.

Is the Odd Fellows connected to Freemasonry?

The Odd Fellows and Freemasonry are separate organizations, although both are fraternal bodies that promote moral values and brotherhood.

What is the meaning of "Visit the Sick, Relieve the Distressed, Bury the Dead, and Educate the Orphan"?

This is one of the historic mottos of the Odd Fellows, reflecting the organization's commitment to charity, compassion, and service to humanity.

Do Odd Fellows help the community?

Yes. Lodges often organize feeding programs, medical missions, relief operations, scholarship programs, fundraising activities, and other charitable initiatives.

Are there membership fees?

Yes. Most lodges require initiation fees and regular dues to support lodge operations and charitable activities.

What should I wear during meetings?

Dress codes vary by lodge and occasion. Some meetings may require formal attire, while others may allow smart casual clothing.

Can members visit other lodges?

Yes. Odd Fellows traditionally encourage fraternal visitation and fellowship among recognized lodges.

What is a Rebekah Lodge?

The Rebekah Lodge is the branch of the Odd Fellows dedicated to promoting fellowship, charity, and service, historically focused on women's membership.

What is the purpose of lodge degrees?

Lodge degrees teach moral lessons, traditions, symbolism, and the principles of the Order through ceremonial presentations.

Why should someone join the Odd Fellows?

People join the Odd Fellows to serve their communities, build lifelong friendships, develop leadership skills, and become part of a respected fraternal tradition dedicated to helping others.
