About Odd Fellows Quezon City Lodge 10

A community of fellowship, service, and mutual support serving Quezon City and nearby communities.

Our Mission

We promote friendship, love, and truth through community service, mutual aid, and personal development. Our lodge supports members and neighbors through charitable projects, educational programs, and fellowship events.

Our History

Odd Fellows Quezon City Lodge 10 was founded to provide a local chapter for members committed to civic service and mutual support. Over the years we have organized community outreach, scholarship assistance, and disaster relief efforts while maintaining a strong tradition of fraternal fellowship.

Regular lodge meetings are held every second Saturday of the month at 3:00 PM. Visitors are welcome by appointment. Membership information and requirements are available from the Secretary.

Values and Programs

We focus on three core values:

  • Friendship — building strong personal bonds among members.
  • Love — supporting members and the community through acts of service.
  • Truth — promoting integrity, education, and transparency in our work.

Programs include community outreach, scholarship assistance, health drives, and disaster relief coordination.
